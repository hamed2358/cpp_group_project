Main Interaction:
The user controls the sound by moving and rotating a physical marker in front of the camera. The distance changes the pitch, while the rotation changes the volume. The sound stops when the marker is no longer detected.

Required Equipment or Software:
A computer with a working camera, a physical copy of our custom marker, C++17, CMake 3.16 or newer, OpenCV 5.0.0 and SFML 3.0.2.

Known Limitations:
The camera calibration was performed using a MacBook Air 2022, so the distance calculation may be less accurate with other cameras. The marker is sometimes not detected correctly, which can briefly stop the sound. The pitch is limited to a distance range of 0.2 to 1.0 meters.
