## AI Use – PoseEstimator

**AI Tool Used**
ChatGPT was used during the development of PoseEstimator.

**How AI Was Used**
I used ChatGPT to better understand how OpenCV’s solvePnP works and how to calculate the marker’s 3D position and distance from the camera. I also used it to discuss the code structure, understand errors, and improve parts of the implementation.

**Verification**
I reviewed the suggested solution to the errors and compiled the project using CMake. I also tested the application with a physical marker to check that the distance estimation worked when moving the marker closer to and farther from the camera.
